# Validation materials

The manuscript reports two expert-evaluation procedures conducted on a three-passage validation subset: two passages from *1984* and one passage from *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone*.

Three linguist experts rated preservation of emotional impact on a five-point scale. In a separate annotation task, two experts assigned Plutchik-emotion intensities and VAD values, after which a consensus annotation was formed. The repository available for this revision contains aggregate validation outputs but does not contain the individual expert rating sheets or passage-level de-identified annotations. Open-release permission for those underlying files is not documented; they are therefore not reconstructed or inferred here.

The aggregate results reported in the manuscript are provided in `validation_summary.csv`. They are preliminary proof-of-concept estimates and should not be interpreted as evidence of broad generalisability.
